01. About

I'm a software engineer who builds with AI-assisted tooling across the full workflow — from first prototype to production-ready systems. I work fast, iterate in the open, and care more about whether something actually ships than how it was built.

My focus is practical software: automation, tooling, and systems that remove manual work and hold up under real use. I design for the person who has to use the thing, not just for the code underneath it. A decade in telecom support taught me that "turn it off and on again" is underrated life advice; a season on construction sites taught me "measure twice, cut once" is the same rule wearing a different hard hat. Both still apply to every deploy I ship.

I also founded FlowsGrow Automation and FOETS.TECH, building automated workflow systems and technical infrastructure for businesses.
